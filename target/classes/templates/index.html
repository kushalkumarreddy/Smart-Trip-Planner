How TripPlanner Works

A realistic, automated workflow built for real travelers

1. Enter Destinations

Dynamically add your starting point and all the places you want to visit with Google Places autocomplete.

2. Calculate Routes

Computes segment-by-segment distances, total trip km, and optimal route ordering (TSP 2-opt heuristic) across Car, Bike, Transit, and Walking.

3. Rank Sightseeing

Recommends top sightseeing spots ranked by distance, ratings, user reviews, and personal interests. Add spots with 1 click!

4. Day-by-Day Schedule

Generates practical itineraries with 20-min buffers, meal breaks, hotel check-ins, and tracks your visited progress in real time.
